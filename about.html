My Story

From Entrepreneur toGlobal Leadership Coach

I've walked the path you're on. I know what it takes to build something extraordinary, to lead through uncertainty, and to transform vision into reality.

My Journey to Impact

Twenty years ago, I was a young entrepreneur with a big dream and zero roadmap. I built my first company from my apartment, working 80-hour weeks, making every mistake you can imagine.

But through the struggles, I discovered something powerful: leadership isn't about having all the answers—it's about asking the right questions and being willing to evolve.

After scaling three successful businesses and experiencing both triumph and failure, I realized my true calling wasn't just building companies—it was empowering other leaders to achieve what they never thought possible.

Today, I've had the privilege of coaching over 500 CEOs, speaking on stages around the world, and authoring books that have touched millions. But the work that matters most? Watching leaders like you break through barriers and create extraordinary impact.
